Current source 0.0.12 update notes (2026-10-13)
• Site filters now use an exclusion list: add a domain or webpage URL to stop automatic recording for it and its subdomains; remove it to restore recording. Saved records stay.
• New feedback page: up to 500 characters, one optional PNG/JPG/WebP image up to 5 MB, optional email and first-submission consent. Failed submissions keep the draft.
• Simplified popup and records search; records can be filtered by title, episode and viewing date, with batch deletion.
• Updated bilingual layout, sync status, shortcut guidance and privacy disclosures.
