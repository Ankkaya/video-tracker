Core Features

Simple, efficient, privacy-first video progress management

🎯

Auto Record

Automatically records playback progress when visiting video pages. Smart threshold filtering avoids invalid records.

⌨️

Shortcut Save

Press Ctrl+Shift+V to instantly record current video without waiting for threshold. Toast notification on success.

🚀

One-Click Resume

Click any record to jump to the corresponding page and automatically seek to the last playback position.

⏱️

Smart Threshold

Default 30 seconds before recording. Customizable: 0 / 10 / 30 / 60 / 120 seconds.

🔍

Search & Filter

Support keyword search and platform filtering to quickly find videos you want to continue watching.

🌐

Custom Sites

Besides built-in platforms, manually add any video website domain. Universal adapter auto-detects HTML5 players.

How It Works

Lightweight and seamless, works quietly in the background

1

Detect Video

Content Script automatically detects video players on current page and matches corresponding platform adapter.

2

Heartbeat Report

Reports playback progress to Background Service Worker every 5 seconds to accumulate watch time.

3

Threshold Save

After watch time exceeds threshold, automatically saves video info and progress to local storage.

4

Resume Anytime

View records in Popup or Options page, click to jump and continue watching.
